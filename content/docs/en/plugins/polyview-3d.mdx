---
title: POLYVIEW 3D
description: Reconstruct a 3D model from video, inside the editor.
---

**POLYVIEW 3D** builds a model from footage, so you can produce an asset
without leaving the editor.

Open **Plugins** in the top bar, then select **POLYVIEW 3D**. The panel has four
tabs down its left edge: **Upload**, **Tasks**, **Results**, and **Account**.

## Upload Assets

Click or drag to upload. Supported formats are MP4 and MOV.

## Reconstruction Settings

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Quality | Fast, Standard, High Quality |
| Remove Background | On, Off |
| Output Type | The format of the generated model, for example SPLAT |
| Task Title | Optional, to identify the job later |

Select **Start Reconstruction**. The panel gives an estimate of 10 to 30 minutes.

Follow progress under **Tasks**, and collect the finished model under
**Results**.
