**POLYVIEW 3D** reconstructs a model from video uploaded in the editor. You can produce the asset in the project that will use it, then position the finished model alongside the other scene assets.

Open **Plugins** in the top bar, then select **POLYVIEW 3D**. The panel has four
tabs down its left edge: **Upload**, **Tasks**, **Results**, and **Account**.

<ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/plugin-upload.png']} />

## Upload Assets

Click or drag to upload. Supported formats are MP4 and MOV.

Walk around the subject while filming, keep it in frame and move slowly enough to avoid blurred frames. Reconstruction matches features between frames, so clear views from different positions help it recover detail; panning from a single spot does not provide the same coverage.

## Reconstruction Settings

| Field | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quality | Fast, Standard, High Quality | How much detail is recovered, and how long the job runs |
| Remove Background | On, Off | Whether surroundings are stripped, leaving the subject alone |
| Output Type | For example SPLAT | The format of the generated model |
| Task Title | Optional | A name to find the job by later |

Turn on **Remove Background** when capturing a single object and the surroundings are not needed. Leave it off for a room or site, where those surroundings are part of the scene you want to reconstruct.

Give the job a **Task Title**. The Tasks list fills up quickly, and untitled jobs
are identified only by a timestamp and an id.

## Running it

Select **Start Reconstruction**. The panel estimates 10 to 30 minutes.

Follow progress under **Tasks**, which shows each job with its status and
completion percentage. Collect the finished model under **Results**.

The job runs server-side, so you can close the panel and keep working. Come back
to Tasks when you expect it to be done.

## Afterwards

The model becomes an asset you position like any other. See
[Position Models](/worldbuilder/en/docs/step-by-step-workflow/4-adjust-model-layout).
