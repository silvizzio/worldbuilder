---
title: Light the Scene
description: Light the models so shape and material are visible.
---

import { ShotGrid } from '@/components/docs'

1. Select **Scene** in the top bar, then open the lighting section.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/6-lighting-s1-1.png']} />

2. Adjust the ambient light until shadowed areas are visible.
3. Enable the sky light to soften harsh contrast.
4. Enable the key light and set its yaw and pitch to move it to one side.
5. Raise the key light intensity until surface detail shows.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/6-lighting-s5-1.png']} />


The model shows visible shadows and surface texture, not a flat shape.
