---
title: Add Markers
description: Pin labels to points on the model.
---

import { ShotGrid } from '@/components/docs'

1. Select **Marker** in the top bar.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s1-1.png']} />

2. Under **Add Marker**, choose **Text Marker**, **Image Marker**, **Video Marker**, or **Data Marker**.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s2-1.png', '/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s2-2.png', '/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s2-3.png', '/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s2-4.png']} />

3. The panel shows *Picker ready: click the model surface*.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s3-1.png']} />

4. Click the point on the model you want to label.
5. Under **Basic Info**, enter a **Name**.

   <ShotGrid images={['/demos/project-center/screens/7-add-markers-s5-1.png']} />

6. Set **Style** to **Text** or **Icon**, and pick a colour.

The marker appears on the model and under **Marker Layer** in the Outline.
