---
title: Render Mode
description: Choose whether scene lighting affects the models, or they show their captured appearance.
---

**Render Mode** sits under **Base Mode** in the Scene Panel.

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Lit | Scene lighting affects the models. Ambient, sky, and key light settings change what you see. |
| Unlit | Scene lighting is ignored. Models show their original textures and colours. |

The panel notes that Unlit suits scanned or baked-texture assets.

A scanned model usually carries the lighting from the day of capture in its
surface. Adding scene light on top produces two sets of shadows, which reads
as muddy. Unlit avoids that.

Use Lit when you want control over how the model presents, or when the model
was produced without baked lighting.
