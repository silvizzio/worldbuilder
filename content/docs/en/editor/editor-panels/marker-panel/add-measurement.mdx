The measurement tools estimate distances and areas from the reconstructed model. Their accuracy depends on the scan, its scale, and the collision proxy used to place the points.

## The three tools

| Tool | What it measures | Points needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Segment | The straight distance between two points | 2 |
| Polyline | The total length along a series of points | 2 or more |
| Area | The area enclosed by a set of points | 3 or more |

## Taking a measurement

Select a tool, then click points on the model.

While you work, the viewport shows a prompt and the status bar counts your
progress, reading something like `Segment measurement: 0 / 2 points selected`.
Press Esc to cancel before finishing.

Completed measurements collect under **Measurements**, which starts empty and
reads "No measurements". The bin icon clears the list.

## When a point lands somewhere wrong

Measurement picks against the collision proxy, not against the surface you can
see. A **collision proxy** is a simplified invisible shape that catches clicks on
the model's behalf, and if it differs from the visible model, your points land on
the proxy rather than where you aimed.

Turn on
[Show collision proxy](/worldbuilder/en/docs/editor/editor-panels/model-panel/collision-proxy)
to see the shape your clicks are hitting and check whether it matches the visible surface.

Accuracy also depends on the reconstruction itself. Measurements are as good as
the scan, so treat them as close rather than as survey data.
